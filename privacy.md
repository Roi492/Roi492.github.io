# Curiosity Paradox Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

This is a private tool used only by the owner of the Curiosity Paradox YouTube channel (@CuriosityParado) and its Instagram account to upload that channel's own videos and read their view counts. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

**What it accesses:** the owner's YouTube channel and Instagram account, their uploaded videos, and those videos' view statistics, through OAuth sign-in.

**What it stores:** video IDs, links and view counts, kept locally on the owner's computer and refreshed at least every 30 days. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

**No other users:** the tool collects no data from viewers or other YouTube or Instagram users.

**Revoking access:** at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) for YouTube, or in Instagram's Apps and Websites settings. Stored data is deleted within 7 days of revocation.
